# Pivot

## What it is
A local, single-user assistant for one ESPN fantasy basketball league. It pulls rankings, stats,
schedules and news daily and tells the manager what to do: lineup, waivers, trades.

## User
One fantasy manager (the owner), category-fluent, checking in once or twice a day on a laptop and
sometimes a phone. Wants the answer first and the evidence one click away.

## Platform
web

## Product truth
- League formats: season-long total points (with games caps), head-to-head points, and category leagues on a points stand-in.
- Core surfaces: Home, This week, Rankings, Trades, Waivers, News, Settings.
- Numbers are the content: FP/G, ranks, games used, projections. Tabular figures everywhere.
- Every recommendation shows its reason in plain language.
- Data is scraped and modelled, so freshness and source status must stay visible.

## Mode
App pages: Operate (dense product UI). Motion conveys state only.
Home: also the public demo's first impression, so it opens with a 3D court the camera flies through on scroll, then hands over to the same dense UI.
